# Central Bank photo credits

The JPG files in this directory are official currency images published by the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan). They are used under the Bank's [Government Website Open Data Announcement](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/cp-307-36676-DE020-1.html), which applies the [Open Government Data License, version 1.0 (Taiwan)](https://data.gov.tw/license) to publicly posted, copyright-protected website materials unless a work is specifically marked as requiring separate consent.

**Attribution:** Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan), source pages linked below. Images are unchanged source files and are only scaled in the page layout. This project is not endorsed by the Central Bank.
